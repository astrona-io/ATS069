# How This Course Is Made

Authors map sections to the official Certified Backstage Associate (CBA) objectives and check mechanisms against primary documentation. They review examples for responsible components, prerequisites and meaningful failure cases.

Structural checks verify reading order, section introductions, closing summaries, draft metadata and YAML question contracts. No runtime execution or laboratory validation is claimed.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS069/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS069/issues), identify the page and disputed statement. The [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS069/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are original practice material. Application names are illustrative and have no special technical meaning.
